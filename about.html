Our Story & Craft

Furniture Made to Live With

We build modern, timeless pieces made to bring comfort, honest materiality, and genuine character into living spaces.

Our Philosophy

Considered Proportions, Honest Materials

Founded on the belief that furniture shouldn't just fill empty floor plan coordinates — it should anchor how a home feels, how light falls across wood grains, and how people rest.

We collaborate with sustainable timber mills and heritage fabric weavers across Europe and India. Every cut of solid white oak, walnut, and tactile bouclé is inspected before crafting begins in our dedicated workshop.

No shortcuts, no throwaway seasonal trends. Just honest joinery and balanced forms that only grow more handsome with age.

Core Standards

What Guides Every Piece

Four enduring principles behind every chair, sideboard, and sectional we produce.

01

FSC-Certified Hardwoods

We source only responsibly managed European oak, American walnut, and kiln-dried timber with full chain of custody.

02

Durable Ergonomic Comfort

We engineer internal cushion densities and seat depths using layered multi-density memory foams that maintain form for years.

03

Non-Toxic Finishes

Every wooden surface is sealed with organic plant-based oils and low-VOC matte sealers that celebrate raw wood grain and touch.

04

5-Year Craft Guarantee

Every joinery joint, mortise, tenon, and structural frame is backed by our full workshop repair and craftsmanship guarantee.
