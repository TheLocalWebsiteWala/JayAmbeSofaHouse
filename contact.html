Frequently Asked Questions

Good To Know

Everything regarding shipping times, trade accounts, and our 5-year structural warranty.

How long does a made-to-order piece take?

In-stock items dispatch within 3–5 working days. Custom upholstered sofas and bespoke timber tables take 2–4 weeks from order confirmation.

Do you offer trade discounts for interior designers and architects?

Yes. We work closely with architects and residential designers. Trade accounts receive tiered volume pricing, expedited swatch shipping, and 3D CAD models.

Can I customize fabric with my own material (COM)?

Yes. We accept Customer’s Own Material (COM) across all sofa and chair designs. Our workshop will confirm yardage requirements upon quote request.

What is covered under the 5-year warranty?

Five full years on hardwood frames, mortise-tenon joinery, and structural springs; two years on upholstery fabric weaves and moving mechanisms.
