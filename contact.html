Frequently Asked Questions

Good To Know

Everything regarding orders, customizations, and our 5-year structural warranty.

How long does custom sofa crafting take?

In-stock models are available for immediate delivery. Made-to-order sofas with custom fabrics and sizing typically take 1–2 weeks from order confirmation.

Can I choose custom fabrics, foams, and dimensions?

Yes. You can select your preferred dimensions (e.g. 3-seater, 5-seater, L-shape), foam density (soft, medium, high resilience), and upholstery textures (bouclé, velvet, leatherette, linen).

Do you provide doorstep delivery and installation in Surat?

Yes. We offer direct doorstep delivery, placement, and installation across Surat and nearby areas.

What is covered under the 5-year warranty?

Five full years on hardwood frames, springs, and joinery structure; plus dedicated service for foam and fabrics.
