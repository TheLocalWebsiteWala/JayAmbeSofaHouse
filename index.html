FAQ

Good To Know

Everything about lead times, delivery and care.

How long does an order take?

In-stock pieces ship within 5 working days. Made-to-order items take 4–6 weeks depending on fabric and finish.

Do you deliver and assemble?

Yes. Delivery includes room-of-choice placement and full assembly, plus packaging removal.

Can I customise fabrics and finishes?

Most upholstered pieces come in 20+ fabrics and four wood finishes. Free swatches on request.

What is the warranty?

Five years on frames and joinery, two years on upholstery and mechanisms.
