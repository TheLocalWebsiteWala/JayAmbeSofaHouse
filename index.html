FAQ

Good To Know

Everything about customization, in-store consultation and warranty.

Can I visit the showroom and test the sofas in person?

Yes! We welcome you to our showroom at Shop No. 23, Green Residency, opposite Dmart, Dindoli, Surat. We are open everyday until 10:00 PM.

Do you offer customized sizing and fabrics?

Yes. Every sofa can be customized in length, cushion firmness, high-density foam, and premium upholstery fabrics (bouclé, velvet, leatherette, and linen).

Do you handle mattress and curtain furnishings too?

Yes, we are a complete furnishing destination offering custom sofas, orthopaedic and luxury mattresses, designer curtains, and interior styling.

What is the craftsmanship warranty?

We provide a 5-year structural warranty on solid frames and joinery, and dedicated support for all upholstery and hardware.
